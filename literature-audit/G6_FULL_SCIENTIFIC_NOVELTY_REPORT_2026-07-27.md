# PHASEMAP G6 full scientific novelty report

**Search date:** 27 July 2026

**Scientific scope:** contract v0.4 Option C claim

**Disposition:** **retain, with narrow wording**

**Evidence status:** broad, documented search of accessible scholarly indexes,
primary records, citation networks, and current accepted/in-press pages; not a
formal systematic review and not proof of universal priority.

## Executive conclusion

No located source contains the complete PHASEMAP result:

1. one two-dimensional finite-inertia active Brownian model;
2. all seven nonempty deterministic reset subsets of position, velocity, and
   orientation;
3. the complete declared component-level second-order record for every map;
4. exact matched-parameter full-record distinguishability for all 21 distinct
   protocol pairs; and
5. a scope-labeled ledger of sector, contracted-observable, conditional,
   resonant, and limiting identities with an explicit separating witness or
   boundary for every restricted identity.

The conjunction is a defensible novelty opening within the literature searched.
The individual ingredients are not independently new. Prior work already
contains complete inertial-active resetting, smaller active and passive
reset-subset taxonomies, position-only and velocity-only underdamped resetting,
retained hidden variables, upstream-sector preservation under downstream reset,
orientation-reset transport, fourth moments, non-Gaussianity, subsystem
resetting, and statistical hidden-state inference.

The appropriate scientific claim is therefore an **exact all-seven
classification and record-relative distinguishability result with restricted
sector structure**. It is not a claim of first partial resetting, first
inertial active resetting, first memory effect, first hidden-state result, or
statistical identifiability. The search supports retaining Option C and does not
support restoring the rejected “higher order breaks a full second-order
equivalence” premise.

Confidence is **moderate to high for the absence of an exact match in
accessible, English-language indexed literature through 27 July 2026**. It is
lower for absolute priority because subscription Web of Science and Scopus
interfaces were unavailable and very recent preprints can be incompletely
indexed.

## 1. Exact claim unit tested

The search treated the novelty unit as the five-part conjunction above, not as
the word “resetting” or any single protocol. This distinction is essential:
nearly every individual behavior in PHASEMAP has a relevant predecessor, while
the exact all-subset comparison and its equality/separation structure were not
located elsewhere.

The claim remains record-relative. “Full record” means the predeclared
component-level second-order record in contract v0.4, at matched
\(M>0,\rho>0,\mathrm{Pe}\ge 0\). It does not mean equality of complete path
measures, all moments, distributions, finite noisy samples, or fitted models.

### 3.3 Broader terminology and mechanism precedents

These sources are not close model matches, but they delimit language:

- Kuśmierz and Gudowska-Nowak, [PRE 99, 052116
  (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.99.052116), and Bodrova,
  Chechkin, and Sokolov, [*Non-renewal resetting of scaled Brownian
  motion*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.05664), distinguish reset rules that erase
  or retain internal age/memory. PHASEMAP must not claim the generic idea of
  retained memory.
- Tal-Friedman, Roichman, and Reuveni, [*Diffusion with Partial
  Resetting*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03363), and Olsen and Löwen,
  [*Partial stochastic resetting with refractory
  periods*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10039), use “partial resetting” for
  fractional reset strength \(x\mapsto ax\). PHASEMAP should use
  **state-coordinate reset subset** when ambiguity matters.
- Majumder, Chattopadhyay, and Gupta,
  [*Kuramoto model subject to subsystem resetting*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14921),
  and later work use “subsystem resetting” for a subset of constituents in a
  many-body system. That terminology should not be claimed or used
  unqualified for coordinates of one particle.
- Sezik *et al.*, [*Conditional splitting probabilities for hidden-state
  inference in drift-diffusive processes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07386),
  use observable events to infer hidden internal states statistically.
  PHASEMAP’s algebraic theorem is not a finite-data inference result, so bare
  “identifiability” or “observability” would invite an incorrect comparison.
- The current review by Keidar *et al.*, [*Stochastic Resetting: A
  Non-Equilibrium Framework for Prediction, Inference and
  Design*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16474) (submitted 17 July 2026), was used
  as a search map rather than as primary novelty evidence. It confirms that
  partial resetting, environmental feedback, inference, and many-body
  resetting are active and fragmented subfields.

## 4. Exact collision test

The closest papers occupy individual rows or faces of the PHASEMAP reset
lattice:

- Patel–Shee occupies \(PV\Theta\) for the same broad inertial ABP.
- Kumar and Ghosh occupy the overdamped \(P\Theta/P/\Theta\) face.
- Baouche occupies \(\Theta\).
- Olsen–Löwen and Howlader *et al.* occupy velocity-reset mechanisms.
- Singh and Capała–Dybiec compare smaller passive \(P/V/PV\) state lattices.
- Chaki–Olsen–Löwen supplies the closest generic sector-preservation
  mechanism.

None of these sources supplies the complete seven-map finite-inertia ABP
classification, the contract-defined component record for every map, all 21
matched-parameter pair separations, or the restricted-identity ledger with
separating witnesses. No forward-citation or accepted/in-press candidate
located in this audit closes that gap.

This supports the central conjunction. It does not support a universal
statement that no such work exists anywhere.

## 6. Recommended publication wording

### 6.1 Headline claim

> We give an exact finite-inertia classification of all seven nonempty
> deterministic reset subsets of position, velocity, and orientation. On the
> declared component-level second-order record, every distinct protocol pair is
> distinguishable at matched \(M>0,\rho>0,\mathrm{Pe}\ge0\), while a
> scope-labeled ledger records the sector, contracted-observable, conditional,
> resonant, and limiting identities that survive below the full-record level.

### 6.2 Literature-qualified novelty sentence

> Within the accessible literature searched through 27 July 2026, we found no
> prior treatment combining a single two-dimensional finite-inertia active
> Brownian model, all seven nonempty position–velocity–orientation reset
> subsets, a complete component-level second-order record, all-21-pair exact
> distinguishability, and a restricted-identity ledger with separating
> witnesses.

### 6.3 Position-toggle structural sentence

> Three matched protocol pairs share the same autonomous internal
> \((\mathbf v,\mathbf u)\) process while differing in their spatial sector,
> providing an exact structural explanation for localization-versus-transport
> separation inside the all-seven classification.

This is defensible as a result within PHASEMAP’s model and comparison map. It
must not be presented as the first example of retained memory, partial reset,
unidirectional sector preservation, or hidden-state structure.

## 7. Required guardrails

- Use **full-record distinguishability** or **exact matched-parameter
  full-record distinguishability**.
- If “record-relative identifiability” appears, define the exact record
  immediately and state that no statistical or practical identifiability result
  is claimed. “Full-record distinguishability” is safer.
- Use **state-coordinate reset subset** when “partial reset” could be confused
  with fractional reset strength or many-body subsystem resetting.
- Use **sector identity**, not “memory channel,” as the formal result label.
  When discussing prior memory-retaining mechanisms, state that PHASEMAP's
  identity is exact invariance of an autonomous internal Markov sector, not
  nonrenewal memory, environmental memory, history-dependent resetting, or
  non-Markovianity.
- Keep full-record equality, contracted-observable degeneracy, sector identity,
  conditional identity, resonant identity, and limiting identity visibly
  distinct.
- Do not infer full protocol equivalence from trace equality, raw MSD equality,
  or internal-sector equality.
- Do not use an absolute “first,” “unique,” or “unprecedented” claim.
- Keep higher-order/distributional work optional and interpretive.

## 8. Recommended scientific disposition

**Retain Option C.** The accessible evidence supports the narrow combined
classification and structural theorem. The contracted \(PV/PV\Theta\) example
can remain as an illustration, not the headline. Removing the secondary claim
entirely would be unnecessarily conservative because the all-seven
distinguishability-plus-identity structure remains unoccupied in the located
literature. Reverting to the contracted-observable route as the main claim
would be weaker and more exposed to prior work on reduced observables and
partial resetting.

The manuscript should add the newly surfaced phase-space subset taxonomies,
the Chaki–Olsen–Löwen upstream-marginal precedent, the inertial AOUP and RTP
neighbors, and the accepted area-exploration paper. Its limitations should
state that the literature search is broad but not systematic and that the
theorem is exact-record, not finite-data, identifiability.

## 9. Residual uncertainty

The remaining uncertainty is bibliographic, not a known scientific
contradiction:

- Web of Science and Scopus were not directly searched.
- Google Scholar citing/cited-by could not be traversed programmatically.
- Indexing for 2026 preprints and accepted papers is incomplete and
  fast-changing.
- Search coverage is strongest for English-language work since the modern
  stochastic-resetting literature expanded after 2011, and specifically for
  2018–2026 phase-space and active-particle work.
- Older control-theory, hybrid-systems, non-English, or terminology-mismatched
  literature could contain a generic analog. Such an analog would most likely
  narrow mechanism language; it would overturn the central claim only if it
  also supplied the same model, seven-map record, all-pair theorem, and identity
  ledger.

Accordingly, this report supports submission-grade narrow wording but not a
patent-style or universal priority assertion.
