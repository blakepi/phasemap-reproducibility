## Closest located work

1. Patel and Shee, [Controlling inertial active Brownian motion via stochastic
   resetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21134) (2026), treats the same broad
   inertial-active setting but resets position, velocity, and orientation
   together. It derives steady moments through fourth order for complete reset.
   It is the closest finite-inertia baseline and directly limits novelty claims
   based on complete reset or fourth moments alone.
2. Kumar, Sadekar, and Basu, [Active Brownian Motion in two-dimensions under
   Stochastic Resetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.03294) (2020), treats an
   overdamped two-dimensional active Brownian particle under
   position-and-orientation, position-only, and orientation-only resetting. It
   already establishes stationarity when position is reset and nonstationary
   transport when only orientation is reset. PHASEMAP must distinguish its
   finite-inertia seven-map result from this three-protocol overdamped
   taxonomy.
3. Ghosh, Mandal, and Chaki, [Anisotropic active Brownian particle in two
   dimensions under stochastic resetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05149)
   (2025), study the same three reset choices for an anisotropic overdamped
   particle and show persistent anisotropy under orientation resetting. This
   further excludes broad novelty claims based only on partial reset or
   anisotropic component moments.
4. Shee, [Steering chiral active Brownian motion via stochastic
   position-orientation resetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12223) (2025;
   published 2026), treats a chiral model with position-orientation resetting,
   lower moments, fourth moments, and excess-kurtosis state diagrams. It
   reinforces that higher moments or non-Gaussianity alone are not a secure
   PHASEMAP novelty boundary.
5. Cleuren and Eichhorn, [Sorting by Resetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19430)
   (2026), uses resetting of velocity components or orientation rather than
   position to sort extended Brownian microparticles. It is not the PHASEMAP
   model or all-seven classification, but it shows that velocity/orientation
   component resetting has a broader contemporary literature context.
6. Baouche, Franosch, Meiners, and Kurzthaler, [Active Brownian particle under
   stochastic orientational resetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06769)
   (2024), derive directed motion and long-time effective diffusive variance
   under orientation resetting. The centered-diffusive orientation-reset
   behavior is therefore prior art, not a stand-alone PHASEMAP novelty.
7. Olsen and Löwen, [Dynamics of inertial particles under velocity
   resetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12685) (2024), show late-time linear
   spatial-MSD growth and derive effective drift/diffusion for inertial
   transport with hidden velocity resetting. Velocity-reset diffusion and the
   general hidden-sector mechanism are not individually new here.
8. Gupta, [Stochastic resetting in underdamped Brownian
   motion](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.07227) (2019), treats a passive
   one-dimensional underdamped particle with position reset while velocity is
   retained and establishes a stationary position law. Position-only
   underdamped localization is therefore an explicit baseline.
9. Franke and Sokolov, [Resetting of underdamped Brownian
   motion](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/qyl8-6q9h) (2026),
   analyze passive underdamped full-reset prescriptions and their stationary
   displacement distributions and excess kurtosis. Passive full-reset
   localization or distributional structure is not a PHASEMAP novelty claim.

## G6 citation-index and network completion — 27 July 2026

The broader audit searched OpenAlex, arXiv primary records, APS published and
accepted pages, publisher/archival records, PubMed, INSPIRE, and general
scholarly web results resolved to primary sources. It traversed accessible
forward and backward networks for the nine works above and checked current
accepted/in-press records. Crossref availability was intermittent; Semantic
Scholar returned HTTP 429; direct Web of Science, Scopus, and Google Scholar
citation-interface access was unavailable.

The strongest additional candidates were:

10. Singh, [Random acceleration process under stochastic
    resetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.05576) (2020), compares complete
    position-velocity reset with position-only reset retaining velocity in a
    passive one-dimensional model.
11. Capała and Dybiec, [Random acceleration process on finite intervals under
    stochastic restarting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.05203) (2021), compare
    position-only, velocity-only, and joint position-velocity resetting using
    first-passage behavior.
12. Chaki, Stølevik Olsen, and Löwen, [Dynamics of a single anisotropic
    particle under various resetting
    protocols](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-648X/ada336) (2025), give a passive
    three-protocol position/orientation taxonomy. Their multivariable appendix
    explicitly shows that resetting a downstream variable leaves an
    unidirectionally coupled upstream marginal unchanged. This is the closest
    generic precedent for the position-toggle sector mechanism.
13. Shankari and Sahoo, [Active Ornstein-Uhlenbeck particle under stochastic
    resetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18515) (2025 preprint), treat an
    inertial active OU particle under resetting.
14. Howlader, Mondal, and Das, [Velocity Resetting of Inertial Run-and-Tumble
    Particles in Non-Newtonian
    Media](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00560) (Physics of Fluids, 2026), treat a
    one-dimensional inertial active particle under velocity-only reset.
15. Shee, [Active Brownian particle under stochastic position and orientation
    resetting in a harmonic trap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06920) (2025),
    derives exact second and fourth moments and excess kurtosis.
16. Olsen, Löwen, and Caprini, [Optimal area exploration by resetting active
    particles](https://journals.aps.org/prl/accepted/10.1103/sytg-m4ms)
    (accepted 29 June 2026), use position resetting in active-particle
    exploration.
17. Baouche and Kurzthaler, [Optimal first-passage times of active Brownian
    particles under stochastic
    resetting](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5SM00340G) (Soft Matter 21,
    5998–6011, 2025), treat an overdamped two-dimensional ABP with joint
    position-orientation reset and first-passage observables.

These additions materially narrow broad claims about partial phase-space
resetting, inertial active resetting, hidden-variable mechanisms, fourth
moments, and position-reset control. None supplies the exact PHASEMAP
five-part conjunction.

## Dated novelty delta

Within the focused search and the broader documented G6 audit, no located
source combines all of the following:

- one two-dimensional finite-inertia active Brownian model;
- all seven nonempty deterministic reset subsets of position, velocity, and
  orientation;
- the complete component-level second-order output record for every map;
- exact full-record distinguishability for all 21 distinct pairs; and
- a scope-labeled ledger of sector identity, contracted-observable degeneracy,
  conditional identity, resonant identity, and limiting identity with
  separating witnesses.

That combination is the narrow opening supported by the accessible literature
searched through 27 July 2026. The strongest structural element is not merely
“seven protocols,” but the exact organization of restricted identities and
separating witnesses inside the all-pair distinguishability map. Position
toggles preserve the full internal `(v,u)` sector while changing spatial
transport to localization, but the generic idea of an unchanged upstream
sector under downstream reset has prior art and is not claimed independently.
`PV`/`PVTheta` is a useful contracted-observable illustration, not a separate
equivalence claim.
