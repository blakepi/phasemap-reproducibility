# PHASEMAP novelty boundary

**Current boundary date:** 27 July 2026 after the focused G4 refresh and the
directly delegated G6 public citation-index, citation-network, primary-record,
and accepted/in-press audit recorded in
`docs/briefs/G6_FULL_SCIENTIFIC_NOVELTY_REPORT_2026-07-27.md`.

## Established and not claimable as new

- stochastic resetting of active particles;
- complete reset of inertial active Brownian position, velocity, and orientation;
- inertial active resetting in the broader AOUP or run-and-tumble sense;
- component resets in overdamped active motion;
- smaller passive position/velocity reset-subset taxonomies;
- position-only and velocity-only resetting in passive underdamped motion;
- directed motion with long-time centered diffusion under orientational
  resetting;
- retained hidden variables, nonrenewal memory, or upstream-marginal
  preservation under downstream reset as generic mechanisms;
- subsystem resetting as a general term or many-body control strategy;
- statistical hidden-state inference or observability as a general topic;
- standard Markov jump generators, renewal methods, and moment hierarchies;
- non-Gaussianity or fourth moments in the complete-reset problem;
- a parameter sweep or seven unrelated curves.

## Provisional opening

A unified finite-inertia all-subset classification, not located in the
accessible literature searched through 27 July 2026, with:

- exact second-order matched transport laws;
- necessary/sufficient stationary-versus-diffusive conditions;
- exact full-record distinguishability across all seven reset maps;
- a scope-labeled ledger of contracted-observable degeneracy, sector identity,
  conditional identity, resonant identity, and limiting identity, with an
  explicit separating witness or boundary for every restricted identity;
- an autonomous internal-sector interpretation and rigorous limiting
  validation.

Use **sector identity** as the formal label. The word “memory” may appear only
when distinguishing this result from prior memory-retaining reset mechanisms;
it is not a claim of first retained memory, nonrenewal memory, environmental
memory, history-dependent resetting, or non-Markovianity.

An optional higher-order/distributional contrast may strengthen interpretation
but is not part of the minimum novelty claim. `PV`/`PVTheta` is eligible as a
contracted-observable-degeneracy example but is not selected by this boundary.

## Claim test

A skeptical reader should be able to state the difference from the complete-reset paper, the overdamped component-reset taxonomy, and passive velocity-reset work after reading one sentence.

The focused G4 search and broader G6 audit located complete-reset
finite-inertia work, inertial AOUP and velocity-reset run-and-tumble work,
overdamped three-protocol position/orientation taxonomies, passive
position/velocity subset taxonomies, orientation-only active resetting,
position-only and velocity-only passive underdamped resetting, chiral,
trapped, and anisotropic reset models, passive full-reset distributional work,
and distinct sorting and area-exploration applications. They also located a
generic unidirectionally coupled reset argument in which resetting a downstream
variable leaves an upstream marginal unchanged. These precedents narrow the
mechanism language but do not supply the exact all-seven finite-inertia
full-record-distinguishability plus restricted-identity structure.

The allowed literature-qualified sentence is:

> Within the accessible literature searched through 27 July 2026, we found no
> prior treatment combining a single two-dimensional finite-inertia active
> Brownian model, all seven nonempty position–velocity–orientation reset
> subsets, a complete component-level second-order record, all-21-pair exact
> distinguishability, and a restricted-identity ledger with separating
> witnesses.

This is bounded search evidence, not a proof of absence or an absolute priority
claim. Subscription Web of Science and Scopus interfaces and the direct Google
Scholar citation interface were unavailable; OpenAlex and other accessible
public indexes, primary records, citation networks, and accepted/in-press pages
were searched instead.

If new prior art occupies the all-seven classification or the exact structural
claim, create a BL5 novelty packet rather than quietly changing the claim. If
an optional higher-order/distributional contrast is retained, assess its prior
art separately.
